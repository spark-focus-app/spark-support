PRIVACY POLICY

Spark Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 31, 2026

Spark ("the app") provides tasks, events, notes, focus records, photos, profiles, friends and teams. This policy explains what information the app handles and why.

Information we handle

  • Display name, profile image and Spark ID that you enter
  • Tasks, events, notes, attached photos, focus time, countdown events, settings and backups
  • Information needed for friends, teams, invitations, shared tasks, comments, blocking and support
  • In-app usage totals such as focus time and completed tasks
  • Your device calendar, if you allow access

How we use it

The information is used to provide the app's features, sync between your devices, back up and restore, share with others, deal with misuse, and answer your questions. The app does not track you for advertising and shows no third-party ads.

Where it is stored and how it syncs

Your data is stored on your device. If you turn on iCloud sync, it is stored in and synced through your iCloud using Apple's CloudKit. iCloud sync for note photos is off by default and can be changed in Settings. Information needed for friends and teams is processed with CloudKit so the people you share with can see it.

Device permissions

The app asks for notifications, photos, camera, calendar or Face ID only when you use a feature that needs them. You can change these in iOS Settings. If you don't allow one, only the related feature may be unavailable.

Sharing with third parties

We do not sell your information, except where required by law. Information you choose to share is shown to the people you share it with. Apple's services are used to store and sync data.

Retention and deletion

Information is kept as long as needed to provide the features, or until you delete it. You can use in-app deletion, Trash, and deletion of account or shared data. With iCloud sync on, deletions reach your other devices on the same Apple Account after syncing.

Children

The app is not intended to collect personal information without consent from users who need a parent's consent. If this applies to you, use the app under a parent's supervision.

Analytics and advertising

This website uses no analytics, advertising, cookies, external fonts or third-party tracking.

Changes

We may update this policy when features or laws change. Important changes will be announced on this page or in the app.

Contact

Email our support address. You don't need to include personal information such as your name, address or phone number.
