Contact

For bugs, data recovery or feature questions, email our support address.

You don't need to send personal information such as your name, address, phone number, note text or photos. It helps if you include the app version, your device and iOS version, the screen where it happened, the steps, any error message and how often it happens.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

iCloud sync isn't working

Make sure you're signed in to iCloud, iCloud Drive is on and you're online. Note photos only sync if "Also save note photos to iCloud" is turned on in Settings.

The widget doesn't update

Open Spark once and save your countdown events or display settings. Updates can be delayed in Low Power Mode.

I don't get notifications

Check that both iOS Settings → Notifications → Spark and the notification settings inside Spark are turned on.

Backup and restore

Use the backup feature in Spark's Settings. We recommend making a backup before switching devices or reinstalling.

Delete my data or shared information

You can use in-app deletion, Trash, stop sharing, and delete account data. If something doesn't work, contact support without including personal information.

Does it cost anything?

The current version has no purchases, ads or feature limits. Every feature is free.

Which languages are supported?

Japanese, English, Korean, Simplified Chinese and Traditional Chinese. You can change the language in Spark's Settings → Language.
